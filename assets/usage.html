App Usage Guide

When you launch the app the very first time and you are not connected to the internet, the app will not work. Once you are connected to the internet, the app will download the home screen from the server with a session lifetime of seven days. This means for the next seven days you'll see your home screen even if you are not connected to the internet.

Button Indicators

The home screen features the following status buttons:

  • Black Button: The assignment is not yet opened by the teacher.
  • White Button: The assignment is opened by the teacher and you are supposed to attempt it now.
  • Grey Button: The assignment is opened by the teacher but you need to attempt previous level(s) to get access to this assignment.
  • Red Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is extremely poor.
  • Light Red Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is poor but better than red.
  • Orange Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is not good.
  • Yellow Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is moderate.
  • Light Green Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is satisfactory.
  • Dark Green Button: You have attempted this assignment and the score is good.

Downloading Assignments

Once you click on any of the buttons, the app will download the assignment with a session lifetime equal to that of the home screen. This means if you have downloaded the home screen on the first day of a month and the assignment paper on the fourth day, both will have session validity until the eighth of the month.

Note: While the assignment is getting downloaded, a Grey Tick will appear next to the button. Once fully downloaded, the tick will turn Green.

Storage Limits & Optimization

You can download a maximum of seven assignments at a time. If you exhaust this quota and try to download an eighth one, the app will prompt you to delete an existing assignment by uploading it to the server first.

Don't worry—you can re-download deleted assignments any time from the server without restriction. The limit exists to optimize memory and bandwidth usage.

Connectivity Recommendations

It is strongly recommended that you remain connected to the internet as frequently as possible. Only when connected will your attempted assignments reach the server and become visible to your teachers. Your score will improve your CGPA only when assignments are uploaded back to the server.

Troubleshooting

If you are facing issues like assignments remaining closed despite being opened by the teacher, or session timeouts, you can use the following Reset options from the menu:

  1. RESET: Application will delete the home screen and download it again. (The app automatically performs this every 7 days).
  2. HARD RESET: Use this if scores are incorrect or assignments remain locked. The server will recalculate your scores from the beginning for each assignment.
  3. TOUGH RESET: Use this only if the previous methods fail or if you see no assignments. This deletes all assignment info and recalculates everything. Note: This cannot be performed from the tab directly.

How to perform a TOUGH RESET

Send an SMS from your registered mobile number to:

880088 4167

Format: ToughReset [13-Digit Enrollment No]

Example: If your Enrollment Number is 1234567890123, send: ToughReset 1234567890123

Important Notes:

  • Registered mobile number can be the Student's, Father's, or Mother's as updated in your profile at www.z7i.in.
  • TOUGH RESET takes approximately one hour. You will receive a confirmation SMS.
  • After receiving the SMS, you must perform a standard RESET from the app menu.
  • TOUGH RESET can only be performed once every 24 hours.
